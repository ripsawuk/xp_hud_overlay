## The alignment

`row(1, ...)` over a box of `-CFG.width/2 .. +CFG.width/2`: the label is centred in the bar's own box and the bar is
centred on the screen, so the text's centre is the screen centre at every length.

The popup is now placed from the centred label's right edge - `x = w/2 + 16`, clamped inside the bar - instead of
`LABEL_X + w`. `TRACKER_MAX` (`-120`, the old "stay left of the middle" guard for the classic HUD's perk icons) is
gone: the popup sits right of centre by design now.

`fitGlow` centres on `0` instead of `LABEL_X + tw/2`.
