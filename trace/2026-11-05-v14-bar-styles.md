## What ships

| Style | Drawing | Pieces at 1000 px |

move. The replay asserts exactly that across every switch.

## The trade-off

A style draws whole glyphs only, so its fill quantises to the pitch (24/26 px ≈ 2.5 % of a 1000 px bar). The line
style stays exact. That is the cost of drawing a pattern rather than stretching one image.

1. XP Bar > Look: each style redraws the fill in place; the text and `+N XP` do not move.

4. Reset bar returns it to the file's style (`line`).
